# Economic Research

## Purpose

Determine whether FOMC announcements have a larger impact on Japanese government bond yields than BOJ announcements and provide recommendations for a corporate treasurer responsible for managing interest-rate and foreign-exchange risk exposure.

## Results

Pending data collection and event-study analysis.

The analysis will compare:

- Japanese 10-year government bond yield reactions to FOMC announcements
- Japanese 10-year government bond yield reactions to BOJ announcements
- USD/JPY exchange-rate reactions to both event types

The results will be used to determine whether Japanese financial conditions appear more responsive to U.S. monetary policy or domestic monetary policy.
