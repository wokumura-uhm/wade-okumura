# Perfect Competition Decision Memo

**To:** Farm Operations Manager
**From:** Wade Okumura
**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Re:** Recommendation for Tomatoes, Carrots, and Mesclun planting mix

---

## Recommendation

My recommendation, based on the constraints we are working with, is to plant 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds. This mix maximizes our profitability because tomatoes are expanded until the marginal cost approximately equals market price, while carrots and mesclun are produced to their maximum allowable limits because both remain profitable at their production caps.

---

## Judgment Call

If additional acreage becomes available, it should first be allocated to carrots rather than mesclun. The shadow-price analysis indicates that relaxing the carrot constraint creates more incremental value than relaxing the mesclun constraint, making carrots the preferred expansion opportunity.

---

## What Would Change the Recommendation

The recommendation is most sensitive to changes in market prices. A decline in tomato prices would reduce the optimal number of tomato beds, while increases in crop prices or relaxation of crop-specific bed constraints could justify a different planting mix.
