The Problem: I want to make a recommendation on a planting plan for tomtoes, carrots, mesclun on 1.5 acres. The resources consist of 64 beds, one pair of hands, and the budget for up to 4 temporary workers. The plan must be committed for the entire season. I will not know the prices, I can only control how much to grow of each crop.

Hypothesis: I expect a relatively balanced planting plan of approximately 22 tomato beds, 21 carrot beds, and 21 mesclun beds to maximize profit. My reasoning is that labor requirements increase as more beds of a single crop are planted, so concentrating too heavily on any one crop may cause diminishing returns to outweigh the additional revenue. A balanced mix may allow the farm to spread labor demands across crops while utilizing all 64 available beds.
